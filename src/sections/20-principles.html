principles

the rules every screen follows

styled, but functional

craft serves use. if a flourish doesn't aid legibility, orientation, or speed, it goes.

always know where you are

fixed nav, sticky headers, and breadcrumbs keep orientation at any scroll position.

tools stay on screen

the controls for the current surface stay visible. reaching an action is never a hunt.

aligned, left-aligned

one axis, one 4/8 grid. labels and values share a left edge; numbers are tabular.

glanceable

icons lead data, not chrome. one family, consistent sizing across providers, tools and states.

maximize usability

usability beats ornament at every fork. comfortable targets, obvious states, aa contrast.
